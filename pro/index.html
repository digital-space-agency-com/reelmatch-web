Questions about Pro

Why is ReelMatch Pro cheaper on the website?

App stores take a commission on in-app subscriptions. Buying on the website avoids it, and we pass part of the saving on to you.

How do I unlock Pro in the app after paying on the website?

Use the same email at checkout as in your ReelMatch account. We email you a link that opens the app and links your purchase to your account. Pro then works on iPhone and Android.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Every receipt email includes a link to manage or cancel your subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

What does ReelMatch Pro include?

Unlimited daily swipes, filtering by streaming provider, genre filters and instant TV launch. Swiping, watchlists, friends and matching stay free.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe. ReelMatch never sees or stores your card details.

Do I need ReelMatch Pro to match with friends?

No. Matching is free for everyone, with 15 swipes a day on the free plan and bonus swipes when you invite friends. Pro is for people who want to swipe without limits and filter by service or genre.
