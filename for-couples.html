Questions

What's the best way for a couple to decide what to watch?

Each person says yes or no to the same options separately, then you watch something you both said yes to. ReelMatch is a free iPhone and Android app that does this with trailers.

Can we use ReelMatch if one of us has an iPhone and the other Android?

Yes. ReelMatch works across iPhone and Android with no extra setup.
