# YouTube monetization plan (27 October 2026)

Status on 21 Oct: 489/500 subscribers, 3/3 uploads in 90 days, 24/3,000 watch
hours (365 days), 444 Shorts views of 3M. Thresholds are from memory, not
re-checked live: confirm in YouTube Studio > Earn.

Two tiers: 500 subs + 3,000 hours (or 3M Shorts views) = fan funding only.
1,000 subs + 4,000 hours (or 10M Shorts views) = ad revenue. Shorts watch time
does not count toward hours.

## Rules

- Original content only. Voiceover and analysis must carry the video; clips
  are seasoning, not the product.
- No dating wording. Say "say yes to", "the titles you both said yes to".
- Say "I built ReelMatch" where the app is mentioned. Only true claims.
- No bought views, no sub-for-sub.
- Put these on a separate channel or playlist from the automated trailer
  channel (@reelmatchapp). Reviewers judge the whole channel.

## The math

3,000 hours = 180,000 minutes. At an average view of 3.5 minutes that is about
51,000 views across all videos. A realistic target is 6 to 12 months, driven by
two or three videos that rank in search, not by ten equal ones. Watch time per
video matters more than upload count: aim for 50%+ retention.

## Trailer content: a safer format

Do not reupload trailers or stitch them with captions. If you use clips:

1. Each video has a point of view: a ranking, a comparison, a "which should you
   start" verdict, with at least 60% your voice and screen time.
2. Clips are short (under about 10 seconds) and used while you comment on them.
3. Prefer the official embed in a website post over a reupload where you can.
4. Expect Content ID claims. A claim usually means revenue goes to the rights
   holder, not a strike, but repeated copyright strikes can end the channel.
5. Never use the automated pipeline for this format: each video needs a human
   script. Mass-produced uploads are exactly what "inauthentic content" targets.

Fair use is a judgment call, not a guarantee. Treat any clip-heavy video as a
risk and keep the originals-only videos as the core of the channel.

## Shorts (supporting, not the goal)

Cut 20 to 25 second Shorts from videos 1, 2 and 6 for discovery and
subscribers. They do not move the 3,000 hours.

## Weekly checklist

- Publish one video; add chapters and the landing page link.
- Check Studio > Analytics > Reach for search terms, and double down on those.
- Reply to every comment for the first 48 hours.
- After video 3, review retention and rewrite the next title and hook.
- Re-check Earn eligibility monthly; apply only when it says eligible.

## Decision points

- After 5 videos: if none passes 1,000 views, change topics, not volume.
- At 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours: apply for ad revenue.
- If monetization stalls, the channel still pays off through AI Overview
  citations and downloads. Track installs from the video link, not just hours.
