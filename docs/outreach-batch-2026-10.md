## Why these targets

Real (non-spam) links on 12 Oct 2026: movie-matcher.com about 15, Matched
about 7, ReelMatch about 5–8. All three were also hit by the same September
spam wave, which can be ignored.

- **movie-matcher.com:** nearly all of its links come from one Lifehacker
  article (Feb 2021). Blogs, The Mirror and local radio morning shows copied it.
- **Matched:** Product Hunt, SaaSHub, El Confidencial and Brad Berens' column.
- **Reelgood, JustWatch and Letterboxd:** among their thousands of links,
  the attainable ones are "best apps to find something to watch" articles.
  Examples: Lifehacker "7 Apps Every Movie Lover Needs", CNET "Find streaming
  TV shows and movies with these five apps", Xataka "Las mejores apps y
  servicios para descubrir series y películas", Engadget media-tracking apps,
  Rich on Tech's favorite apps, Fox 6 Milwaukee "These apps reveal the best
  movies and shows".
