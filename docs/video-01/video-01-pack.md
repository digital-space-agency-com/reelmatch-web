## Script

On-screen text in caps. Edit the wording to sound like you; keep the structure.

**0:00 Hook (10 s)**
"If you're trying to find a TV show to watch with your husband, wife or partner,
and you've scrolled for forty minutes, this is a five-minute fix."
SCREEN: streaming app scrolling, then stop. TEXT: FIND A TV SHOW TO WATCH TOGETHER

**0:10 Why it fails: tone, not genre (60 s)**
"The problem usually isn't genre. It's tone. One person wants light, the other
wants intense, and a title and a one-line description never settle that.
Second problem: whoever suggests a show feels judged when it gets turned down."
SCREEN: two text cards, LIGHT vs INTENSE. TEXT: IT'S TONE, NOT GENRE

**1:10 Method 1: the private shortlist (75 s)**
"Each of you writes down two shows you'd happily start tonight, without showing
the other. Only pick from the overlap. If there's no overlap, each adds one
more."
SCREEN: simple graphic of two lists overlapping. TEXT: PRIVATE LISTS, THEN OVERLAP

**2:25 Method 2: the episode-one deal (60 s)**
"A series is a commitment. Agree before you start that you'll give episode one
a fair go before anyone vetoes it."
SCREEN: talking head or text card. TEXT: EPISODE ONE DEAL

**3:25 Method 3: let an app do the overlap (90 s)**
"If you'd rather not do that by hand, I built a free app called ReelMatch. Each
of you swipes through trailers on your own phone: yes or skip. Nobody has to turn
down the other person's pick. Then it shows only the shows and movies you both
said yes to, and where each one is streaming."
SCREEN: real app, simulator footage: trailer deck, yes, skip, matches screen.
TEXT: YOU EACH SAY YES OR SKIP / ONLY THE OVERLAP

**4:55 Who it works for (30 s)**
"It works for couples, friends and families, and iPhone and Android users can
use it together. It doesn't stream anything itself."
SCREEN: App Store and Google Play badges.

**5:25 Close (20 s)**
"Free on iPhone and Android, link in the description. Pick by the trailer, not
the title."
SCREEN: end card, reelmatch.app, next-video card.
