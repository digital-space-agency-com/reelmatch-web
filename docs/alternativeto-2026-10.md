Listing: https://alternativeto.net/software/reelmatch/

**Problem (9 Oct 2026):** ReelMatch is filed under "Movie Review Sites". Its alternatives are therefore review and recommendation sites (ratehouse, TasteDive, Criticker, MovieLens, FilmAffinity…). Only Cineswipe is a real peer. People browsing the Reelgood, JustWatch or Letterboxd alternatives pages never see ReelMatch, even though AlternativeTo ranks #5 on Google for "reelgood alternative".
