# AlternativeTo listing fixes (Oct 2026)

Listing: https://alternativeto.net/software/reelmatch/

**Problem (9 Oct 2026):** ReelMatch is filed under "Movie Review Sites". Its alternatives are therefore review and recommendation sites (ratehouse, TasteDive, Criticker, MovieLens, FilmAffinity…). Only Cineswipe is a real peer. People browsing the Reelgood, JustWatch or Letterboxd alternatives pages never see ReelMatch, even though AlternativeTo ranks #5 on Google for "reelgood alternative".

Sign in to AlternativeTo with the account that added ReelMatch (or claim the listing via "Are you the developer?"). Edits are reviewed by moderators, usually within a few days.

## 1. Edit the ReelMatch listing

**Short description** (shown in lists)

```
Can't agree on what to watch? Everyone swipes through movie and TV trailers on their own phone, and ReelMatch shows the titles you all said yes to. Free on iPhone and Android.
```

**Full description**

```
ReelMatch helps couples, friends and families agree on a movie or show in minutes.

Everyone swipes yes or skip on trailers on their own phone, and ReelMatch shows the titles you all said yes to, so nobody has to veto anyone. Trailers, not posters, so you can judge the tone before you commit.

- QuickMatch: swipe together in real time with one person
- Works for two people or a whole group
- iPhone and Android users can use it together
- Covers titles on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Apple TV+, Max and more
- Free; ReelMatch Pro adds streaming-service and genre filters and launch on your smart TV

ReelMatch doesn't stream anything itself. Movie and TV data from TMDB, trailers from YouTube.
```

**Application types:** keep **Movie Review** and **Movie Database**, and leave the others unticked. AlternativeTo has no "movie discovery" type. Reelgood itself is filed as "Movie Review Site" + "Movie Database" (checked 9 Oct 2026), so these two types are what put ReelMatch next to Reelgood, JustWatch and the swipe apps. The type list it offers is built from the apps ReelMatch is already linked to, which is why only these appear. "Social Network" and "Media Manager" would file it next to the wrong apps.

What actually changes which apps show next to ReelMatch is the features/tags (below) and the "alternative to" links in section 2.

**Features / tags to add** (pick from AlternativeTo's existing tags where they exist):
- Movie Recommendations
- Watch together / Group decision making
- Swipe interface
- Trailers
- TV show recommendations
- Streaming service guide
- Cross-platform (iPhone + Android)
- Ad-free (only if true for the free tier)

**Platforms:** iPhone, iPad, Android. **License:** Freemium. **Website:** https://reelmatch.app
